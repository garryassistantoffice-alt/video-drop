Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode ten: the stone guest. Candles, a laid table, a little band playing the hits of the day, and then a tune Leporello knows only too well, from The Marriage of Figaro. Leporello steals pheasant. Ah, what a barbarous appetite, laughs his master. Donna Elvira bursts in and kneels. She asks only that he change his life. He laughs, and drinks: long live women, long live good wine! She goes to the door, and screams. She runs out the other way. Leporello looks out and comes back white. The stone man, he says. Ta ta ta ta: those are his steps. A knock. Leporello hides under the table. The Commendatore fills the doorway. Don Giovanni, you invited me to supper, and I have come. Will you come to supper with me? Don Giovanni does not run. I will come. Give me your hand on it. He gives it. What cold is this? Repent! No! Repent! No! The statue lets go. Fire comes from every side, the floor opens, a chorus from below: all this is little for your crimes. Who is tearing my soul? he cries. Don Giovanni goes down into the abyss. The others come too late. Donna Anna asks Ottavio for one more year. Elvira goes to a convent. Zerlina and Masetto go home to dinner. Leporello, to the inn, to find a better master. And all of them sing: this is the end of those who do evil.
