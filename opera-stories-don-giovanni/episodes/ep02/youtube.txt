Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode two: the catalogue. Dawn on a street outside the city. Leporello, brave for once, tells his master the truth: your life is the life of a scoundrel. Don Giovanni only smiles, and hushes him. He smells something. A woman. A lady in a travelling dress comes out of the inn, singing Ah, chi mi dice mai: who will tell me where that monster is? The man who promised to marry her and broke his word. If I find him, she sings, I'll tear his heart out. A lady in distress, says Don Giovanni. Let us go and console her. He bows, she turns, and they are face to face. It is Donna Elvira of Burgos. He came to her house, called her his wife, and left after three days. She has followed him across Spain. He listens, unmoved. Then he tells Leporello: you tell her everything. And he is gone. Leporello pulls out a long book. Madamina, il catalogo è questo: little lady, here is the list of the women my master has loved. In Italy, six hundred and forty. In Germany, two hundred and thirty-one. A hundred in France. Ninety-one in Turkey. But in Spain, already one thousand and three. He praises a blonde for her kindness, a dark-haired one for her faithfulness. Countesses, maids, a baroness, a farm girl: rich or poor, old or young, as long as she wears a skirt, you know what he does. Elvira stands alone in the street, and swears revenge.
