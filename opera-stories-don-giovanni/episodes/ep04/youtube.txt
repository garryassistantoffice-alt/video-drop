Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode four: that voice. Donna Anna, in black mourning, walks with Don Ottavio. They meet a friend, a nobleman of the town: Don Giovanni. They ask him for help in finding her father's killer. Of course, he says. My sword, my house, my heart are yours. Then Donna Elvira appears. Non ti fidar, o misera: don't trust him, poor lady, that wicked heart has betrayed me. Don Giovanni lowers his voice to the others: the poor girl is mad. Elvira swears she is not. Anna and Ottavio look from one to the other. Don Giovanni leads Elvira away before she makes a scene. Before he goes, he turns back to Donna Anna. If you need me, he says, I am yours. Goodbye. She goes white. Don Ottavio, I am dying, she whispers. That voice. That is the voice of the man in the cloak. That is the man who killed my father. And she tells Ottavio the whole night. It was late. She was alone in her room. A man in a cloak came in, and in the dark she thought he was Ottavio. Then he seized her. She fought, she screamed, she tore herself free and followed him out, and her father came. Or sai chi l'onore: now you know who tried to take my honour, who killed my father. I ask you for revenge; your own heart asks it. Ottavio cannot believe that a nobleman could do such a thing. He will watch Don Giovanni, and find the truth.
