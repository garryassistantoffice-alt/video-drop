Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode seven: under the window. Leporello has had enough. I'm leaving, he says, you nearly got me killed. Come now, you clown, says Don Giovanni, and hands him four gold coins. Leporello stays. Just one thing, he says: give up women. Give up women? his master laughs. Women are more necessary to me than the bread I eat, more than the air I breathe. Who is faithful to one, he explains, is cruel to all the others. And tonight he wants Elvira's maid. Servants trust servants, so the two men change clothes: Leporello gets the cloak and the hat with the white plumes. Donna Elvira stands at her window in the evening. Ah, taci, ingiusto core: be quiet, unjust heart, don't beat for him. Hidden behind Leporello, Don Giovanni sings up to her: forgive me, Elvira, come down. Leporello waves his arms to match the words. I repent, he sings, I'll die if you don't believe me. Leporello can hardly keep from laughing. That lying mouth is at it again, he mutters; heaven protect her. And Elvira, against all reason, against everything she knows, believes him and comes down. Leporello, in his master's cloak, leads her away into the dark, terrified she will look at his face. Don Giovanni stays under the window with a mandolin. Deh, vieni alla finestra: come to the window, my treasure, come and take away my tears. Your mouth is sweeter than honey, he sings. A light moves in the maid's room. Someone is coming. He sings softer, and waits in the dark below the balcony.
