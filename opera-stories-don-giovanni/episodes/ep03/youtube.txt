Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode three: the peasant bride. A country road, a village wedding. Giovinette che fate all'amore: young girls in love, don't let the time slip by. Zerlina, in a wreath of flowers, and her groom Masetto dance with their friends. Don Giovanni sees the bride. What a lovely face. He invites them all to his palace: Leporello, give them chocolate, coffee, wine and ham. Everyone except the bride, who stays with him. Masetto refuses. Don Giovanni touches his sword. Masetto understands at once: Ho capito, signor sì, I've understood, yes, my lord. And he goes, warning Zerlina that she will be sorry. Alone with her, Don Giovanni says a pretty face like hers is not made for a peasant. He will marry her himself, today, in his little house nearby. Là ci darem la mano: there we will give each other our hands. Zerlina wavers: Vorrei e non vorrei, I would and I would not. My heart trembles a little. Then, together: Andiam, let us go. Two steps from the gate, a voice: Stop, villain! It is Donna Elvira. She tells the girl everything: Ah, fuggi il traditor, run from the traitor, don't believe his words. Then she takes Zerlina by the hand and leads her away, leaving Don Giovanni alone on the road, for once without a woman.
