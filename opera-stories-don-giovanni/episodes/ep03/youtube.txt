Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode three: the peasant bride. A country road, a village wedding. Young girls in love, the villagers sing, don't let the time slip by. Zerlina, in a wreath of white flowers, and her groom Masetto, a big farmer, dance with their friends. Don Giovanni sees the bride. What a lovely face. He invites them all to his palace: Leporello, give them chocolate, coffee, wine and ham. Show them the gardens. Everyone except the bride, who stays with him. Masetto refuses: Zerlina cannot stay here without him. Don Giovanni touches his sword. Masetto understands at once: I've understood, yes, my lord. And he goes, warning Zerlina that she will be sorry. Alone with her, Don Giovanni says a pretty face like hers is not made for a peasant. Those roguish eyes, those lovely lips. He will marry her himself, today, in his little house nearby. There, he sings, we will give each other our hands. Zerlina wavers: I would and I would not. My heart trembles a little. Masetto would be so unhappy. Then, together: let us go. Two steps from the gate, a voice: Stop, villain! It is Donna Elvira. She tells the girl everything: run from the traitor, don't believe his words. His lips lie, his eyes deceive. Then she takes Zerlina by the hand and leads her away, leaving Don Giovanni alone on the road, for once without a woman.
