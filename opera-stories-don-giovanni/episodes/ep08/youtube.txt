Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode eight: the wrong man. Instead of the maid comes Masetto, with village men armed with muskets and clubs. They are hunting Don Giovanni. In Leporello's clothes he joins the hunt. Half of you go that way, he orders, half this way. Masetto, you stay with me. He takes Masetto's musket and pistol, and beats him with them. Then he is gone. Zerlina hears Masetto groaning and comes with a lantern. Oh, my head, he moans, my shoulders. Vedrai, carino: you'll see, my darling, if you are good, I know a cure. It costs nothing, no chemist has it. She takes his hand and puts it on her heart. Feel it beating. That is the cure. In a dark courtyard, the real Leporello is still trying to lose Donna Elvira. Alone in this dark place, she sings, my heart is pounding. He finds a door, but Donna Anna and Ottavio come in by it. He turns: Zerlina and Masetto. In the plumed hat, they all take him for Don Giovanni. Kill him, they cry. No, begs Elvira: he is my husband, have pity. Leporello falls to his knees and pulls off the hat. Ah, pietà, signori miei! Perdono, perdono! Forgive me, it's only me! A servant, not a master. While they stare, he slips away. Now Ottavio knows, and decides: he will go to the law. Il mio tesoro: meanwhile, comfort my treasure, dry her tears. I am going to avenge her, and I will come back with news of his death.
