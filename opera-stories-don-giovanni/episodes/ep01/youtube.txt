Full narration
Don Giovanni, episode one: the man in the cloak. Night in Seville. In the courtyard of the Commendatore's palace, a servant paces with a lantern. Leporello. He grumbles about toiling night and day for a master who never thanks him. Bad food, little sleep, he complains. I want to be the gentleman, he says, not the servant. Then he hears footsteps and hides. Out of the house runs a man wrapped in a long cloak, hiding his face. Clinging to him is Donna Anna, the Commendatore's daughter. Don't hope to escape, she cries, unless you kill me. Help! Who is this man? He will not say. She will not let go. Then her father comes, an old man with a sword and a light. Fight me, he says. The stranger refuses: he will not fight an old man. The Commendatore insists. Two blades in the dark, a few strokes, and the old man falls. What a crime, whispers Leporello. Before he dies, the stranger watches him, almost curious. Then he and Leporello slip away into the night. Donna Anna comes back with her betrothed, Don Ottavio, and finds her father in his blood. She faints. When she wakes, she pushes Ottavio away: go, cruel man. He is the only one left to her, he answers, a husband and a father. Then swear, she says. Over the body they swear revenge on a man whose face nobody saw.
