# Episode 1 on painted cutout stages (new, 30 October 2026)

Episode 1, "The room next door", remade the new way. Every painting is now a small theatre: the people stand as cut-out
cards in front of the room, the camera turns a few degrees so they slide against it, and the window light casts their
shadows. Nothing was repainted: the cards are cut from the paintings themselves, and what a moving part uncovers was
rebuilt from the painting around it. The narration is Liam's new recording with the Italian names.

The old episode 1 is still in `../episodes/`; nothing there has changed. If you choose this one, upload these three
files instead of the ones in `episodes/` (same steps as in `../README.md`).

## What moves, shot by shot
1. **The room** (title): the Count at the door turns his head towards the bed, his lace jabot sways, he breathes.
   The bed stands in front, so the room slides behind it as the camera turns.
2. **Figaro measuring**: he looks up at Susanna and nods, his hair sways; her head tilts and her mirror sways.
3. **The room again**: the Count leans in and turns his head towards the bed.
4. **Susanna, your new picture 3**: she glances towards the door; on "Now he regrets it, and wants it back from
   her" her finger wags twice at the knuckle, wrist with it, and once more at the end of the line. Behind the door
   the Count cranes his neck and leans in; her curls, the curtain and the flowers move.
5. **Figaro with the guitar**: he turns his head one way, then the other, raises his brows, strums.
6. **Marcellina** lifts the letter, raises a brow; she and Bartolo move their heads.

61 seconds, 1080 x 1920, made with `stage2d.py`, which draws the same stage Blender builds (see `../blender/`).
