# Opera Stories: The Marriage of Figaro in 12 episodes

All 12 episodes are finished on painted cutout stages (remade on 30 October 2026, replacing the earlier versions). In
every shot the people stand as cut-out cards in front of the room, the camera turns a few degrees so they slide
against it, the window light or the lanterns cast their shadows, and heads, hands and props move; nothing is
repainted (`blender/README.md`). Narrator: Liam, with the Italian names. Each episode is a vertical video under 62
seconds, so YouTube treats it as a Short.

## For each episode, three files
- `NN-title.mp4`: the video to upload
- `NN-title-youtube.txt`: the title (first line) and the whole description (everything below it), with chapters,
  the famous number, the characters and the credits
- `NN-title-captions.srt`: the exact narration with times, to upload under Subtitles (better than automatic ones)

## Uploading in YouTube Studio
1. Upload the mp4. Paste the first line of the `-youtube.txt` file as the title and the rest as the description.
2. Under "Altered content", answer **Yes** (the paintings and the voice are made with AI).
3. Subtitles: upload the `-captions.srt` file (language English, "with timing").
4. Put all 12 in one playlist, "The Marriage of Figaro in 12 episodes", in order.
