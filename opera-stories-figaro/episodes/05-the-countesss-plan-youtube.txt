The Marriage of Figaro, episode five: the Countess's plan. Upstairs, the Countess is alone. Years ago the Count climbed balconies for her. Now he chases her maid. She sings Porgi, amor: love, give me back my husband, or let me die. Figaro has a plan. He has sent the Count an anonymous letter: tonight, during the dance, the Countess meets a lover. Let the jealous husband worry about his own wife for once. And Susanna will promise to meet the Count in the garden at dusk. But it won't be Susanna who goes. It will be Cherubino, in a dress, and the Countess will catch her husband in the act. The Count is out hunting, so there is time. Cherubino arrives and sings his song for the Countess, Voi che sapete: you ladies who know what love is, tell me, is this love? Susanna dresses him up: a lady's cap, eyes down, hands just so. The Countess notices that his officer's commission has no seal yet. Then, a knock. The Count is back early, with the letter. And in his wife's locked room stands a boy with half his clothes off.
