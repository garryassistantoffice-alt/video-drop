Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode four: off to the army. Figaro arrives with villagers dressed in white, throwing flowers: thank you, noble Count, for abolishing the old right! It's a trap. Figaro holds out a white veil: please, sir, put it on my bride yourself, here, in front of everyone. The Count can't refuse in public. He smiles, praises them, and asks for a little delay, for a grander ceremony. Quietly he adds: find Marcellina. And the page who heard too much? The Count forgives him, and makes him an officer in his regiment. He must leave at once. Figaro whispers to the boy: I must talk to you before you go. Then he teases him with Non più andrai: no more fluttering about, little butterfly. Now it's moustaches, muskets, and marching through the mud. But Cherubino is not leaving the palace. Upstairs, the Countess is alone and heartbroken. Next episode: the Countess's plan.
