Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode ten: the letter with the pin. The Countess, alone, remembers: Dove sono, where are the beautiful moments of sweetness and love? She will win her husband back. She dictates, Susanna writes, Sull'aria: what a gentle breeze tonight, under the pines. The letter is sealed with a pin; the Count must send the pin back as his answer. Village girls bring flowers to the Countess. Among them a shy one: Cherubino in a dress. Antonio pulls off the bonnet. Caught again! The Count wants to punish him, but Barbarina speaks up: you kissed me, sir, and promised me whatever I want. I want Cherubino as my husband. The Count is trapped by his own promises. The double wedding begins with a fandango. Kneeling for her bridal wreath, Susanna slips the Count the letter. He pricks his finger on the pin, drops it, then picks it up and pins it to his sleeve. Figaro sees and laughs: some girl has sent the Count a love note. He has no idea it was his bride. Next episode: Figaro's jealousy.
