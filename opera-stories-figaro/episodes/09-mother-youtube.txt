Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode nine: Mother! The verdict: pay her or marry her. Two thousand silver pieces, and Figaro doesn't have them. One last trick: I'm a gentleman, he says. I can't marry without my noble parents' consent. Robbers stole me as a baby, dressed in gold, jewels and embroidery. And I have a mark on my arm. A little spatula, on the right arm? asks Marcellina. How do you know? Because you are my son, Raffaello. And your father is standing right there: Doctor Bartolo. Just then Susanna runs in with a purse: a thousand gold coins to set Figaro free. And she sees him hugging Marcellina. Slap! Your mother? Yes, his mother! In the famous sextet everyone explains, Susanna laughs, and Marcellina and Bartolo decide to marry as well. Two weddings today. The Count leaves, beaten. But tonight his wife will wait for him in the garden in Susanna's dress, and Figaro knows nothing about it. Next episode: the letter with the pin.
