Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, the whole story in short episodes. Episode one: the room next door. Near Seville, about 1786. Figaro, the Count's valet, marries Susanna, the Countess's maid, today. He is measuring their new bedroom, a gift from the Count. Perfect, says Figaro: if the Count rings at night, I'm there in two steps. Susanna is not charmed. And when the Count sends you away on an errand, she says, he's at my door in three steps. Because the Count, bored with his wife, now wants Susanna. He once abolished the old lord's right to a servant's bride. Now he regrets it, and wants it back from her. Her own singing teacher, Basilio, carries his messages. Figaro sees the plan. The Count is going to London as ambassador: Figaro will be his courier, always away, with Susanna close at hand. So Figaro sings Se vuol ballare: if the Count wants to dance, I'll play the guitar. He will act the fool and turn every trick around. But Figaro has another enemy. Marcellina, the housekeeper, holds a paper with his signature. If it stands, Susanna is not the one he marries.
