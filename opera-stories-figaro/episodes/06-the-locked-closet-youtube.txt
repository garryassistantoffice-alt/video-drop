The Marriage of Figaro, episode six: the locked closet. Cherubino runs into the Countess's dressing closet. Pale, she lets her husband in. Why was the door locked? Then a crash from the closet: the boy has knocked over a chair. Who is in there? Only Susanna, says the Countess, trying on her wedding dress. In the trio Susanna, or via, sortite, the Count orders her to come out, and the Countess forbids it. The Count has had enough. He will break the door down. He locks every door, and takes his wife with him to fetch the tools, so nobody can slip away. But Susanna saw it all from the alcove. The moment they leave, she lets the boy out. The only way out is the window, above the gardener's flowerbeds. Cherubino jumps. Susanna locks herself in the closet in his place. The Count returns with a hammer and pliers. Now the Countess confesses: it's Cherubino, half undressed, but innocent, just a harmless joke. The Count snatches the key. Let him die! He turns to the door. Next episode: the door opens.
