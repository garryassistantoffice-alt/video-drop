Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode eleven: Figaro's jealousy. Night. Barbarina searches the garden with a lantern. She has lost the pin the Count gave her for Susanna, with a message: the seal of the pines. Figaro hears it, and his world falls apart. His bride, on their wedding day, has a meeting with the Count. Marcellina doubts it, but Figaro won't listen. He hides among the pines to catch them, and sings Aprite un po' quegli occhi: men, open your eyes and see what women really are. Susanna and the Countess arrive, already in each other's clothes. Marcellina has warned Susanna that Figaro is hiding nearby. So Susanna teases him. In Deh vieni, non tardar, one of the loveliest arias Mozart wrote, she sings: come, my love, don't be late. Figaro thinks it is for the Count. It is for him.
