The Marriage of Figaro, the last episode: Contessa, perdono. In the dark the Countess waits in Susanna's dress. Cherubino finds her, takes her for Susanna, and tries to steal a kiss. The Count steps between them and gets the kiss himself. He swings at the boy; the slap lands on Figaro, creeping closer. Then the Count woos "Susanna", really his own wife, and gives her a ring. Figaro meets "the Countess", but he knows that voice: it's Susanna. He plays along and declares his love to "my lady". Slap! Then they laugh and make up. The Count sees Figaro at the feet of "his wife" and explodes. He calls everyone out. Everyone begs: forgive! No, no, no, he shouts. Then the real Countess steps forward. The Count understands, and kneels: Contessa, perdono, Countess, forgive me. She does: I am kinder than you, and I say yes. The crazy day ends in a party. That was The Marriage of Figaro.
