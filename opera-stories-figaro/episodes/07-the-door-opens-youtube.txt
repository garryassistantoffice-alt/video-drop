Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode seven: the door opens. Out steps Susanna. Calm, smiling: my lord, why so surprised? The Count is lost. The Countess is just as stunned, but quickly plays along. Now the Count must beg forgiveness. Rosina, he says, her name from the old days. And the letter about a lover? Figaro wrote it, the women admit. Only a joke. Figaro bursts in: the music is ready, let's have the wedding! Then Antonio the gardener, Susanna's uncle, half drunk, with a smashed pot of carnations: a man jumped from this balcony onto my flowers! It was me, says Figaro, rubbing his ankle. Then you dropped this paper, says Antonio. The Count grabs it. What is it? The women whisper the answer: Cherubino's officer's commission. And why did you have it? Because, says Figaro, it needed the seal. Saved again. But then Marcellina, Bartolo and Basilio march in with the contract, and the Count smiles. He will judge the case himself. Next episode: the trap in the garden.
