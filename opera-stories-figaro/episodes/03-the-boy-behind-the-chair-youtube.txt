Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode three: the boy behind the chair. Yesterday the Count found Cherubino alone with Barbarina, the gardener's daughter, and sacked him. Now he begs Susanna: ask the Countess to save me. He adores the Countess. He snatches her ribbon from Susanna's hand and sings Non so più: I don't know what I am any more; every woman makes my heart race. Footsteps: the Count! Cherubino hides behind the big armchair. The Count sits, takes Susanna's hand and asks her to meet him in the garden at dusk. Then Basilio's voice outside. Now the Count hides behind the chair; the boy slips round onto the seat, and Susanna throws a dress over him. Basilio gossips: everyone knows the page is in love with the Countess. The Count jumps out. What does everyone say? Yesterday at Barbarina's, he says, I lifted the tablecloth, like this, and found the page! He lifts the dress. There is the page, again. Worse: the boy was there the whole time, and heard everything the Count said to Susanna. Next episode: off to the army.
