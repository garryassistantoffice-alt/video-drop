Full narration
The Marriage of Figaro, episode eight: the trap in the garden. The Count paces. A locked closet, a letter, a jump from a window. Is he being played? The Countess has a new plan, and this one she keeps even from Figaro. Susanna will promise the Count a meeting in the garden tonight, but the Countess herself will go, in Susanna's clothes. Susanna finds the Count. Will she come? Yes, she says, if he gives her the dowry he promised, so she can pay off Marcellina. Their duet is Crudel, perché finora: cruel girl, why make me wait so long? He is delighted. But leaving, Susanna whispers to Figaro: you've already won your case. The Count hears it. A trap! In Vedrò mentr'io sospiro he rages: shall I sigh while my servant is happy? Never. Figaro will marry Marcellina. And the judge, Don Curzio, is ready with the verdict. Next episode: Mother!
